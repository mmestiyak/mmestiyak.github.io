Hi, I'm Meer.

You can't cram a harvest. You can't skip feeding cattle. You can't fake a fight camp. Software is no different — real products come from patient, daily, unglamorous work. That's how I build.

I also build my own things: Kommercly, a WhatsApp-first store builder made for entrepreneurs like the ones I grew up around in Bangladesh — because I'm not just the developer of that customer, I am that customer.

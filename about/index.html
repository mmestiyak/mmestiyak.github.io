Hi, I'm Meer.

Full-stack engineer, five years in production: Node.js, React, Next.js, TypeScript, Postgres, plus hardware integration work at IDEEZA. Remote from Bangladesh. I also run a cattle and silage farm, which is where most of what I believe about software came from.

Open to senior full-stack roles, remote, from Bangladesh. resume

I’m from Bangladesh, the country of rivers.

At seventeen, I needed a way to support my family, test my potential, and carve out a path. That’s when I found software engineering. Through countless stormy, lonely, sleepless nights of learning I kept grinding, never entirely sure where it would lead. Sustained by the belief of my family and friends, that restless teenage necessity turned me into a self-taught engineer. Eventually, it worked out better than I ever thought. Today I work full-time and remote as a software developer at IDEEZA, building software that reaches into hardware: custom Blockly blocks that compile to Arduino and Raspberry Pi firmware, and a no-code APK generator. Before that, at JoulesLabs, I built the front end of FundedNext, a prop trading firm, and Crowdfundly, an enterprise SaaS fundraising platform, and went from intern to leading the frontend team in eight months. Beside the job, my brother and I build software businesses of our own. The current one is Kommercly, a WhatsApp-first store builder (a lighter Shopify, you could say) that turns your Google Sheet into a live store in minutes. Stop building, start selling.

But the land pulled me back, too. I co-founded Khamarvest with a few others, an agro-business seeded by the trust of my colleagues. What started as an idea is now a reality of cattle farming and maize silage production running side by side. The dream is a village of integrated farming where tradition meets modern engineering, and the land gives everything it can.

Underneath all of this is my true compass: Islam. I believe my life is an Amanah, a sacred trust from Allah, and I know I will one day stand before Him to answer for how I used my time, my youth, and my abilities. I am not a perfect Muslim, but I am constantly trying to shape myself through learning and repentance.

My faith defines my vision for success. It teaches me that wealth is a tool, not a destination. I am working to build an honest, solid income, not for luxury but to free myself from the endless worldly race. I deeply believe in minimalism, because the things we own eventually own us. I want to build enough wealth to be completely free: free to focus on my true purpose, do good for my people, and keep my life simple and light.

I believe discipline must exist in every part of life. My adventures take me on long cycle rides deep into the countryside, navigating muddy village roads and stopping at small roadside dokans and bustling local bazars. The raw beauty of Bangladesh out there is incredible. It often feels like I am cycling straight through the vibrant, painted landscapes of a wall calendar. Beyond the roads, I am actively training in MMA and have already stepped into the cage for two club wins. My physical goals don’t stop there. I dream of becoming a master swimmer, while horseback riding and marksmanship are waiting their turn.

When I look to the future, I dream of traveling the world: not skimming the surface as a tourist, but immersing myself and living like a local. Ultimately, my mission is simple. I want to build unbreakable strength in every single dimension of life: physically, mentally, spiritually, and financially.

And this is my cloud home. Logs when I feel like writing, moments when something is worth showing, work when it ships. Just a life lived intentionally, and shared simply.

How I work

No skipped days

Cattle, fight camps, and production systems all fail the day you skip. So I don't skip.

From database to deployment; from seed to silage to doorstep. I own the whole path, not a slice of it.

Simple survives

Minimal code, minimal business, minimal life. Whatever cannot stay simple eventually breaks, or breaks you.

Built for seasons

Products that outlive their launch week, a farm that outlasts the season, fitness that outlasts one fight. The long run is the only run.

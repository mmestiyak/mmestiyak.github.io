Khamarvest

Agro farm built with a small founding team: cattle and maize silage production delivered nationwide, growing toward tech-integrated farming

Co-founder · 2024 · Operating

live ↗ silage ↗

What it does

  • ·15 cows and counting, in a farm built for 18
  • ·Seeded with money raised from my own software colleagues
  • ·Maize silage production and nationwide delivery, feeding the Khamarvest herd first, then other farms
  • ·WhatsApp-first ordering for silage customers, like everything I build
  • ·The long dream: a village of integrated farming where traditional agro meets IoT

Khamarvest is the oldest kind of production system (animals, feed, seasons) run with the same rule as software: show up every day, because none of it can be faked.

Alongside the cattle, we grow and chop maize, ferment it into silage, and deliver honest feed to farms across Bangladesh, our own herd first, then others. Inventory, logistics, and seasonal demand, the same problems software startups have, with heavier bags.

The long dream is a whole village of integrated farming, where traditional agriculture meets sensors and automation, and a farm reaches everything it could be.

What it involves

Cattle, Maize silage, Feed planning, Nationwide delivery, Seasonal cycles

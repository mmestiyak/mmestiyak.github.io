This is a sample collection. A collection is a folder holding one `index.md`
and as many photographs as you like — thirty, a hundred — plus the story that
holds them together. Write the story here; the photographs appear below it as
a contact sheet, and each one opens full size.

Delete this folder once you have a real set. To keep a collection off the
index page, out of the sitemap and away from search engines — reachable only
by its link — add `unlisted = true` to the frontmatter.

Kommercly is a no-code WhatsApp store builder that turns a Google Sheet into a live store in minutes. Built for entrepreneurs in Bangladesh and beyond who want to start selling online without the complexity of traditional ecommerce platforms. Features include WhatsApp-first ordering, real-time inventory from Sheets, and 0% platform fees.

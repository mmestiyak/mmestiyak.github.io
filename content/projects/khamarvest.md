Khamarvest is the oldest kind of production system (animals, feed, seasons)
run with the same rule as software: show up every day, because none of it can
be faked.

Alongside the cattle, we grow and chop maize, ferment it into silage, and
deliver honest feed to farms across Bangladesh, our own herd first, then
others. Inventory, logistics, and seasonal demand, the same problems software
startups have, with heavier bags.

The long dream is a whole village of integrated farming, where traditional
agriculture meets sensors and automation, and a farm reaches everything it
could be.

I'm from Bangladesh, the country of rivers.

At seventeen, I needed a way to support my family, test my potential, and carve out a path. That's when I found software engineering. Through countless stormy, lonely, sleepless nights of learning I kept grinding, never entirely sure where it would lead. Sustained by the belief of my family and friends, that restless teenage necessity turned me into a self-taught engineer. Eventually, it worked out better than I ever thought. Today I work full-time and remote as a software developer at [IDEEZA](/work/), building software that reaches into hardware: custom Blockly blocks that compile to Arduino and Raspberry Pi firmware, and a no-code APK generator. Before that, at JoulesLabs, I built the front end of FundedNext, a prop trading firm, and Crowdfundly, an enterprise SaaS fundraising platform, and went from intern to leading the frontend team in eight months. Beside the job, my brother and I build software businesses of our own. The current one is [Kommercly](https://kommercly.com), a WhatsApp-first store builder (a lighter Shopify, you could say) that turns your Google Sheet into a live store in minutes. Stop building, start selling.

But the land pulled me back, too. I co-founded [**Khamarvest**](https://khamarvest.com) with a few others, an agro-business seeded by the trust of my colleagues. What started as an idea is now a reality of cattle farming and maize silage production running side by side. The dream is a village of integrated farming where tradition meets modern engineering, and the land gives everything it can.

Underneath all of this is my true compass: Islam. I believe my life is an _Amanah_, a sacred trust from Allah, and I know I will one day stand before Him to answer for how I used my time, my youth, and my abilities. I am not a perfect Muslim, but I am constantly trying to shape myself through learning and repentance.

My faith defines my vision for success. It teaches me that wealth is a tool, not a destination. I am working to build an honest, solid income, not for luxury but to free myself from the endless worldly race. I deeply believe in minimalism, because the things we own eventually own us. I want to build enough wealth to be completely free: free to focus on my true purpose, do good for my people, and keep my life simple and light.

I believe discipline must exist in every part of life. My adventures take me on long cycle rides deep into the countryside, navigating muddy village roads and stopping at small roadside _dokans_ and bustling local bazars. The raw beauty of Bangladesh out there is incredible. It often feels like I am cycling straight through the vibrant, painted landscapes of a wall calendar. Beyond the roads, I am actively training in MMA and have already stepped into the cage for two club wins. My physical goals don't stop there. I dream of becoming a master swimmer, while horseback riding and marksmanship are waiting their turn.

When I look to the future, I dream of traveling the world: not skimming the surface as a tourist, but immersing myself and living like a local. Ultimately, my mission is simple. I want to build unbreakable strength in every single dimension of life: physically, mentally, spiritually, and financially.

And this is my cloud home. Logs when I feel like writing, moments when something is worth showing, work when it ships. Just a life lived intentionally, and shared simply.
